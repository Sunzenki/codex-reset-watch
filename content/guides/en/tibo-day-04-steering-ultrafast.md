An active task may be changing the wrong area or missing a constraint. Prompt correction can reduce wasted work. [Day 4](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2108275041276420573) targets that interaction: Tibo says steering is now instant, allowing the model to respond sooner to a change of direction during execution.

## Steering changes an ongoing task

For example, while several files are being edited, you might add “handle only the login page first” or correct a misunderstood requirement. These illustrate the announced interaction. Changes already made to files or external systems still need review.

This differs from [Day 14’s editing of earlier messages](/guides/tibo-day-14-edit-messages/): steering adds direction during execution, while editing an old message returns to an earlier prompt position.

## Ultrafast’s comparison and scope

The same post quotes @OpenAIDevs: GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast began rolling out that day to the API, Codex and ChatGPT Work. The official performance claim is **up to 8× Sol Standard’s speed**. Tools and workload still affect whole-task duration.

A plan-availability question under the post identifies a useful distinction. The announcement names product surfaces, without a complete subscription eligibility table. Check whether your account can select the mode, then consult its applicable usage or billing guidance. Unverified prices in replies are not used here.
