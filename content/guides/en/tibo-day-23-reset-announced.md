The Day 23 usage reset has landed. Tibo quoted his earlier timing reply and [confirmed completion](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2115330127886463244) on **October 28 at 06:29:38 UTC**. That is the public confirmation time; he did not give the exact backend execution time.

Earlier, [Tibo’s Day 23 post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2115273064481218829) called it a reset day, and his [follow-up](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2115273253711409208) said it would land by midnight PST.

## The earlier midnight PST estimate

The announcement was posted on October 28 at 02:42:53 UTC and the reply at 02:43:38 UTC. It was still the evening of October 27 in Pacific time, so the [series calendar](/guides/tibo-28-day-plan/) files Day 23 under October 27 and interprets midnight as the end of that day.

“By midnight” was a deadline, not an exact execution time. Tibo has since confirmed completion, so the countdown has ended; the table preserves the earlier estimate.
