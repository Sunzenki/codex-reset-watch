## Check receipt before redeeming

[OpenAI’s reset guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001498-how-resets-work-in-codex) distinguishes a saved banked reset from an automatic usage refresh. On desktop, open Settings → Usage & billing, then History under Usage limit resets. “Reset received” records a grant; “Reset used” records redemption. Check Available for what remains usable and its expiry.
