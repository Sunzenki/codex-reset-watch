At 07:26 UTC on November 26, Tibo posted that the **GPT-6.2 compensation banked reset is available**. The earlier announcement said users on **Go, Plus, Pro, and Business** would each receive one banked reset. The confirmation gives no expiry date for the grant. [Tibo’s availability confirmation](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2125853628250599730)

Earlier that day, Tibo said OpenAI had fixed a launch error that made GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol count less toward most users’ plan allowances than intended. [Tibo’s announcement on X](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2125784147889393806)

## How the fix affects your usage

The same work now draws more from your allowance. Compared with last week, Tibo says users will reach their limits **40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra** and **25% faster on GPT-6.2 Sol**. Those figures describe how quickly users hit the limit. Tibo expects both models to still go further than their respective predecessors, GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol. He says other models were unaffected and OpenAI will not charge the difference for past usage.

## Where to find your banked reset

Open **Settings → Usage & billing** in the desktop app or **Usage settings** on the web. Check **Available** for the reset. Review which allowances it covers and when it expires, then choose when to use it. Your usage allowance refreshes when you redeem the reset.
