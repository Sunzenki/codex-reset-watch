[Tibo’s plan](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2106845241357824205) was to ship an update with a clear benefit for most Codex / Work users, or perform a full usage-limit reset, every day for 28 days. This calendar starts with Day 1 on October 5, 2026, in US Pacific time, and ends on November 1.

## How dates and categories are recorded

The [Day 17 reset](/guides/tibo-day-17-reset-announced/) has landed, verified on the site owner’s account. The article preserves the earlier announcement and deadline.

The [Day 18 usage reset has also landed](/guides/tibo-day-18-reset-announced/). Its original announcement and deadline conversion remain available in the article.

[Day 19](/guides/tibo-day-19-small-updates/) covers faster dot calls, reasoning-cache reuse and CLI changes. [Day 20](/guides/tibo-day-20-reset-announced/) announces another reset by midnight PST: October 25 at 08:00 UTC under literal PST, or 07:00 if PDT was intended. Completion is not yet confirmed.

Open a day to read its changes, conditions, original posts and relevant questions. “Covered” means an individual article is available. Day 3 remains a banked-reset announcement; Day 15 is classified as a broadcast and reset; Day 17 is a usage reset verified on the site owner’s account; Day 18 has also landed. Day 19 is a feature day; Day 20 is a reset announcement awaiting confirmation. Later days will receive articles when their updates can be verified.

Calendar dates follow US Pacific time (PT), including daylight saving changes. Daily articles separately list important post times in UTC. Lists show the site’s actual publication date. Series dates remain in article details and this calendar; this index’s header uses the plan’s start date. Day 9 retains the distinction between the post’s last edit time and its original publication time.
