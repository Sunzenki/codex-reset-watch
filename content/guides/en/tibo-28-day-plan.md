[Tibo’s plan](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2106845241357824205) was to ship an update with a clear benefit for most Codex / Work users, or perform a full usage-limit reset, every day for 28 days. This calendar starts with Day 1 on October 5, 2026, in US Pacific time, and ends on November 1.

## How dates and categories are recorded

Open a day to read its changes, conditions, original posts and relevant questions. “Covered” means an individual article is available. Day 3 remains a banked-reset announcement; Day 15 is classified as a broadcast and reset. Later days will receive articles when their updates can be verified.

Calendar dates follow US Pacific time (PT), including daylight saving changes. Daily articles separately list important post times in UTC. Lists and article headers use the calendar’s series date; this index uses the plan’s start date. Day 9 retains the distinction between the post’s last edit time and its original publication time.
