[Tibo’s Day 25 post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2115850175868727621) announces increased limits for Pro 100, Pro 200, Pro 500 and Business Premium, effective that day. [OpenAI’s updated Pro guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers) gives important detail: for Work and Codex, **Pro 100 and Pro 500 receive twice their previous allowance, while the result for Pro 200 depends on which allowance the account already had**.

## What changes for each plan

| Plan or seat | Work and Codex allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Pro 100 and Pro 500 | Twice the previous included allowance, according to OpenAI. |
| Pro 200, previously higher allowance | The announced reduction is canceled; the higher allowance stays the same. |
| Pro 200, previously lower allowance | The lower allowance doubles to match the higher allowance. New Pro 200 subscriptions receive that same allowance. |
| Business Premium | Tibo announces doubled limits; the post does not give a detailed per-product breakdown. |
| Plus | OpenAI’s Pro help page says its usage is unchanged. |

OpenAI says existing subscribers receive the updated Work and Codex allowance automatically without waiting for renewal. All Pro 200 subscriptions now have the same allowance, and the price remains $200 per month. **Keeping the higher Pro 200 allowance does not mean it doubles again.**

## Why the Pro 200 clarification matters

In [his earlier announcement](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2104823812042940713), Tibo described a new Pro 200 calculation that would produce roughly half the previous API-dollar-equivalent budget. The help center later described a temporary transition for eligible existing subscribers. OpenAI now says the reduction announced in its September 29 email is canceled. The [earlier CRW Pro 200 article](/guides/pro-200-grandfathered-allowance-october-2026/) has been corrected to reflect this change.

The updated Pro guidance also separates **Chat** from Work and Codex: Pro 100 and Pro 500 retain their current weekly GPT-6 Pro message limits in Chat; the lower Pro 200 Chat limit rises to match the higher one. A higher subscription allowance does not guarantee twice as many completed tasks: model, context and settings affect consumption.

## What about dot usage?

Tibo says extended dot limits will continue for now and dot usage remains separate from default usage; he gives no new end date. The [dot documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots) distinguishes conversations with your dot from tasks it starts or manages in Work or Codex: those delegated tasks count toward the respective Work or Codex limits. Do not assume every task a dot launches is outside your allowance.

Check your own [usage dashboard](https://chatgpt.com/codex/settings/usage) and account or workspace. This is a plan-allowance change, not an announced one-time reset; it also does not change the separate [subscription API credit benefit](/guides/chatgpt-pro-business-api-credits/).
