## Plans, usage windows and reset type

Neither post lists eligible plans, identifies the usage windows affected or says a banked reset will be issued. A request for a banked reset in the replies is a user request, rather than evidence of the delivery method.

[OpenAI’s one-time reset guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11369540-using-codex-with-your-chatgpt-plan) explains that eligibility, covered limits and redemption conditions can differ between offers. This announcement therefore does not establish a permanent allowance increase or guarantee a reset button that can be saved for later.

## Check your own allowance

Open your [usage dashboard](https://chatgpt.com/codex/settings/usage), or enter `/status` in an active CLI session. Check the account and workspace you are using, the allowance name, remaining usage and any reset time shown. The official help article above lists these lookup methods.

For a before-and-after comparison, keep timestamps for the same account and allowance; one account’s change describes that account.
