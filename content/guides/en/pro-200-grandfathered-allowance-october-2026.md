OpenAI has **canceled the planned reduction** to Pro 200’s Work and Codex allowance. Its [updated Pro guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers) says accounts already using the higher allowance keep it; accounts on the lower allowance have it doubled to match. All Pro 200 subscriptions now receive the same allowance, including new subscriptions, at the existing $200 monthly price.

## What changed from the earlier plan

The prior OpenAI guidance said eligible existing subscribers could keep their previous allowance through October 29, 2026, before moving to a lower allowance. In [his earlier post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2104823812042940713), Tibo described the newer calculation as roughly half the old plan’s API-dollar-equivalent budget. OpenAI now says the reduction in its September 29 email is canceled. This replaces the earlier October 29 transition described in this article.

For someone already on the higher Work and Codex allowance, the effect is to **keep that allowance**, not double it again. Someone on the lower allowance moves up to the higher level. OpenAI says existing subscribers receive the change automatically, with no need to wait for renewal or cancel and resubscribe. Its [Day 25 announcement](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2115850175868727621) also covers other plans; see the [Day 25 guide](/guides/tibo-day-25-pro-usage-allowance/).

## Check the right limit

Use [Settings → Usage](https://chatgpt.com/codex/settings/usage) to check the account and workspace you actually use. Work and Codex share included allowance; task cost varies with the model, context, reasoning and settings. Chat’s weekly GPT-6 Pro message limit is separate: on Pro 200 the lower limit rises to the higher one, while the previously higher limit stays the same.

The [API credit benefit](/guides/chatgpt-pro-business-api-credits/) is separate: it does not increase signed-in Codex limits or cover Codex usage through API keys. A reset refreshes eligible windows and does not itself change the plan allowance.
