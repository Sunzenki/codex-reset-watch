[OpenAI’s Pro tier guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers) says eligible existing Pro 200 subscribers may retain their previous included allowance **through October 29, 2026**, while their subscription is active. After that date, they move to the lower included allowance; the price remains $200 per month.

## Who qualifies

The transition applies to an account that had an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from September 22 through **10:00 a.m. Pacific Time on September 29, 2026**. OpenAI says the same account can qualify after resubscribing if it met that earlier condition and its Pro 200 subscription is active. A new subscriber who does not qualify receives the updated allowance.

The official wording gives October 29 as a date, but does not specify an exact hour or time zone for the later changeover, or quantify the reduction. Do not treat midnight in a particular zone or a percentage reported by another user as an official guarantee. OpenAI says it will email affected users with further details.

## Check your own account

Review your plan, the affected-user email if you receive one, and [Settings → Usage](https://chatgpt.com/codex/settings/usage). Work and Codex share included usage; consumption varies with the model, task and settings. A temporary reset refreshes eligible usage and does not permanently restore the former plan allowance. See the [usage guide](/guides/codex-usage-dashboard-and-shared-limits/) for how to compare windows.

Separately, eligible subscribers can claim [API credits](/guides/chatgpt-pro-business-api-credits/), but that benefit does not increase signed-in Codex limits and cannot be used for Codex via an API key.
