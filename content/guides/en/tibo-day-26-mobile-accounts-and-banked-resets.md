[Tibo’s Day 26 post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2116405414921384099) says mobile users can switch accounts and previews **banked resets for the next two days**. These are Day 27 and Day 28 in the series; the post does not confirm either grant has arrived.

## Switch accounts on mobile

[OpenAI’s account guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001068-using-multiple-chatgpt-accounts-on-web-and-mobile) says the iOS and Android rollout supports two signed-in accounts, with one active at a time:

1. Open Settings, then Switch account.
2. Choose Add account and sign in.
3. Return to Switch account to select the account you need.

If two are already signed in, log out of one before adding another. The option may not have reached your account yet. Conversations, memory, files, billing and subscriptions remain separate; switching accounts does not merge them.

Tibo says the desktop app is coming soon. The official guide already supports the web; web availability does not establish desktop-app availability.

## The next two days are banked-reset days

The post was published on October 31 at 05:42:26 UTC, still October 30 in Pacific time. In the [series calendar](/guides/tibo-28-day-plan/), the next days are **Day 27, October 31 PT**, and **Day 28, November 1 PT**. Exact delivery times, eligible accounts and expiration are not given, so CRW records the announcement without a countdown.

[OpenAI’s reset guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001498-how-resets-work-in-codex) explains that receiving a banked reset saves an opportunity for later use; it does not immediately refresh usage. Once one is available, review its covered allowances and expiry in desktop Usage & billing or web Usage settings before redeeming it. Mobile currently has no reset controls. Account switching is a separate feature.
